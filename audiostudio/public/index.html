Free books from Project Gutenberg: classics that are out of copyright in the US (mostly published before 1930). Describe a book and the AI works out which one you mean. For a newer book you own, bring in your own copy as a file.

The writing

The proofreader and the sentence checker go over every section before anything is recorded: spelling, grammar, run-on and tangled sentences, quotes and who is speaking. Untick it to read the book exactly as written.

        Settings

        Grown-up books

        When this is off, Mature and Explicit books, stories and levels are hidden here and in StoryMaker. It's the same switch in both apps. Hiding them needs nothing; showing them again needs your password.

        Only the owner can show or hide grown-up books.

        Draw Things (pictures)

        Draw Things forgets its API Server switch every time it closes. The studio checks every minute and turns it back on by itself.

        To let it, open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility and turn on “Python” (or Terminal, if you started the studio in a Terminal window). Then press the button below.
